Method & safeguards

Expected pace = mean of team paces. Away efficiency = league baseline + (away adjusted ORtg − league baseline) + (home adjusted DRtg − league baseline). Home efficiency follows the inverse matchup. Scores = expected pace × efficiency / 100; half the home-court advantage is added to the home score and half subtracted from away. League baseline is fixed at 115 for this prototype, not an automatically updated NBA estimate.

Historical ratings source: RealGM 2025–26 NBA Advanced Stats (regular-season full-year team totals), https://basketball.realgm.com/nba/team-stats/2026/Advanced_Stats/Team_Totals/Regular_Season . These ratings are not a 2026–27 forecast and may be stale. No win probability, calibrated confidence, betting EV, injury impact, travel/rest effects, or validated backtest is claimed. A 2-point market difference is only a provisional directional lean, not a profitable edge. This release loads completed-game data written by a scheduled GitHub Actions updater; it is not a live in-game feed. Verify CSV timestamps and exclude post-tipoff data before using historical forecasts.
